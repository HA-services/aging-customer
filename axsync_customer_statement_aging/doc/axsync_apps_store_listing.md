## Full product description
AXSync Customer Statement Aging gives every customer and vendor a clear statement of account: open items, six aging buckets, total due and overdue amount, with your message and payment details. Print one PDF for everyone, export to Excel, or email each partner their own statement. Switch on the schedule and Odoo sends them every week or month.

Problem it solves: statements are built by hand at month end, sent late or not at all, and customers dispute balances because nobody can show them a clean document.

Who should use it: accountants, credit controllers, finance managers and owners of companies using Odoo 18 Invoicing.

How it works: open Invoicing > Statements, choose customers or vendors, choose the partners, check the preview and print, export or send. Or enable automatic sending in the settings and choose weekly or monthly, a minimum balance and overdue customers only. Every email is recorded in a Statements Sent log.

Statement types: Open Items, and Balance Forward with an opening balance, a running balance and a closing balance. Any statement date can be used, so the position of an earlier day can be reproduced.

What is different from a plain follow-up letter: it covers all customers and vendors at once, ages every item, exports to Excel, sends by email with the PDF attached, runs on a schedule, and keeps a log. It needs only Invoicing and Discuss, not Sales or Purchase.

## Feature list

- Customers and vendors
- Six aging buckets from the due date or the invoice date
- Statement as of any past date
- Wizard with live preview of the first statement

- Print PDF, Export Excel (summary plus a sheet per partner), Send by Email
- Aging Report with list, pivot and graph
