# Support

Published by AXSync, www.axsync.com.

## How to get help

1. Email hunain@axsync.com, or use the Contact the Author option on the Odoo Apps Store page of this product.
2. Include: Odoo version and edition, module version, whether Odoo.sh or self-hosted, what you expected, what happened, and a screenshot or the server log lines if there is an error.

## What is covered

Defects in the delivered module and questions about how it behaves. Custom layouts, extra aging buckets, other Odoo versions and other applications are separate work.

## Reporting a defect

Describe the steps to reproduce. For a wrong figure, send the partner, the statement date, the statement type and aging basis, and the invoice and payment numbers involved. For an email problem, send the entry shown in Invoicing > Statements > Statements Sent.
