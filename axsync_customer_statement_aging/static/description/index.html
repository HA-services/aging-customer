What This Module Is For

A statement of account is a single page that shows a customer all open invoices, credit notes and payments, how old each item is and the total due. It is what customers ask for, what auditors expect and the easiest way to start a payment conversation.

Odoo can print an invoice or a follow-up letter, but it has no ready statement that you can run for all customers, age by bucket, send by email and schedule. This module adds exactly that, for customers and for vendors, using only Invoicing and Discuss.

The Business Problems

Customers say they never got a statement

Statements are built by hand at month end, sent late, or skipped when the team is busy.

Nobody knows who is overdue by how much

A list of invoices does not show who is 30, 60 or 90 days late.

Collections starts too late

Without a regular reminder, overdue balances grow quietly.

Hours of copy and paste

Exporting ledgers, formatting a sheet and emailing each customer takes an afternoon.

Disputes about the balance

A customer and your books disagree, and there is no clean document from a given date to settle it.

Suppliers want to reconcile too

Vendors send their statements, but you cannot easily send or read your own side.

Feature Breakdown

1. Open item and balance forward statements

Open Items lists what is unpaid. Balance Forward shows the opening balance, the charges and credits of a period with a running balance, and the closing balance. Choose per run or set a default per company.

2. Customers and vendors

The same statement works for the money customers owe you and the money you owe vendors. Vendor statements use the payable side and are never sent with your bank details.

3. Aging in six buckets

Current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 91-120 and 120+ days, counted from the due date or the invoice date. Shown on every statement, in the aging report and in the Excel file.

4. Statement as of any date

Pick a past statement date and the module rebuilds the position of that day. Payments reconciled later are shown as open again, which settles most balance disputes.

5. Wizard with live preview

Choose who, what and when and see the first statement before anything is printed or sent. Select partners, take everyone with a balance, only overdue partners, and set a minimum balance.

6. PDF, Excel and email from one place

Print one combined PDF, download an Excel file with an aging summary and a sheet per partner, or email every statement with its PDF attached.

7. Automatic sending

Weekly or monthly, with a minimum balance and an option for overdue customers only. A customer is not sent the same statement twice on the same day. Partners can be excluded.

8. Smart email text

Customers with overdue amounts get a polite payment reminder with the amounts. Others get a plain statement. Your message and payment details are added.

9. Statements Sent log

Every email is recorded with partner, recipients, date, amounts, source and the PDF. Partners without an email address and failures are listed under Needs Attention.

10. Aging Report

List, pivot and graph views of the aging for the whole selection, with totals. Filter, group and export with the standard Odoo tools.

11. On the partner form

A Statement button, a Statements tab with receivable, overdue and payable balances, a statement email override, an exclude option, the last statement sent and the history.

12. Multi-currency and multi-company

Foreign currency documents are shown in the company currency with the original amount if enabled. Settings, statements and logs are per company.

How It Works

  1. Install the module. Install Customer Statement Aging from the Apps menu.
  2. Set your defaults. Open Invoicing > Configuration > Settings > Customer Statements. Choose the statement type, the aging basis, a message and your payment details.
  3. Open a statement run. Go to Invoicing > Statements > Customer Statements. Pick the partners and check the preview.
  4. Print, export or send. Use Print PDF, Export Excel or Send by Email, or open the Aging Report for the overview.
  5. Check the log. Invoicing > Statements > Statements Sent shows what was queued and which partners had no email address.
  6. Switch on the schedule. Turn on Send Statements Automatically and pick weekly or monthly, a minimum balance and whether only overdue customers should receive it.

Known limitations
  • The aging buckets are fixed at 30, 60, 90 and 120 days.
  • Figures are in the company currency. Foreign currency items show their original amount when enabled.
  • Scheduled sending covers customers. Vendor statements are sent from the wizard.
  • Journal items without a partner are not included.
  • Queued for Sending means the email was handed to the Odoo mail queue. Delivery depends on your mail server.
  • A past statement date uses the date of the later document of a reconciliation to decide whether an item was paid by then.
  • Labels and texts are in English and can be translated with the standard Odoo tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it send anything after installation?

No. Automatic sending is off until you switch it on. Manual sending asks for confirmation.

What does Queued for Sending mean?

The email was handed to the standard Odoo mail queue with the PDF attached. It leaves when your outgoing mail server processes the queue.

Which partners are included?

Customers or vendors with a posted receivable or payable balance in the company. Contacts of a company are grouped under the company. Documents without a partner are not included.

Can I send a statement for last month?

Yes. Set the Statement Date to the day you need. Payments made after that day are shown as still open.

Where does the email go?

To the Statement Email on the partner if filled, otherwise to contacts of type Invoice Address, otherwise to the partner email.

Can a partner be left out?

Yes. Tick Exclude From Statement Runs on the Statements tab of the partner. Scheduled runs skip them.

Are the aging buckets configurable?

No. They are fixed at 30, 60, 90 and 120 days.

Do I need Sales or Purchase?

No. It needs Invoicing and Discuss only, and works from the posted journal items.

Does it support several currencies and companies?

Yes. Amounts are shown in the company currency with the original amount if enabled. Settings and logs are per company.
